Renders next to the real thing

Each Cycles render (left) comes from a Blender camera placed to match one of our photos (right). The scene is procedural: Python builds the house, porch, cairn, mannequins and all twelve skeletons. Every skeleton has its own rig, so you can repose them in Blender.

Take it apart

The .blend is fully editable. Select Scene_Controls to change the red colour, turn the candles and windows up or down, or slide from night to day. Every camera shows its reference photo as a background. The build scripts recreate the scene from scratch: change a number and run python3 build_scene.py with Blender 5.x.
